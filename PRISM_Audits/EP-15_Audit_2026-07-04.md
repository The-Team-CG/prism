| AC | Verdict | Evidence |

| 1. 2FA mandatory for ALL PMCM Admin via TOTP only, no alternative | **FAIL, confirmed absent — CRITICAL regression on a Done story** | `AuthOperationsService.login()` (`auth-operations.service.ts:34-107`) checks only: user existence, permanent/temporary lockout, password (via `AuthSecurityService.verifyPassword`, scrypt-based), and approval status. **No TOTP/second-factor step exists anywhere in this method or anywhere else in `apps/api`.** A repo-wide, case-insensitive search for `totp`, `2fa`, `twoFactor`, `authenticator`, `otpauth`, `speakeasy` returns exactly one hit outside documentation: `apps/client/src/app/components/ui/input-otp.tsx` — a generic shadcn/ui OTP **input primitive**, confirmed via a further search to be **imported and used nowhere else in the entire codebase** (only its own declaration matches). | 2. **New finding: PA-229 (HIGH).** Password reset with a 15-minute link expiry does not exist anywhere in the codebase.

**New finding: PA-230 (HIGH).** Per-role configurable session timeout does not exist anywhere in the codebase.

**New finding: PA-231 (MEDIUM, ties to PA-69).** Minimum password requirements have zero code representation — no length, complexity, or character-class enforcement exists at any point in the password lifecycle, independent of which exact values Ms. Mys eventually confirms.

## STORY 5: EP-15-S05 — ZDR confirmed and DataSanitizer enforced (CRITICAL GATE)

**Files checked:** `apps/api/libs/modules/ai-governance/src/services/ai-governance.service.ts` (full file, `sanitizePayload`/`runDryRun`), `apps/api/libs/modules/ai-governance/src/services/ai-governance.service.test.ts` (full file), `apps/api/libs/modules/ai-governance/src/services/pare/data-sanitizer.service.ts` (full file — the class actually imported by `PAREService`), `apps/api/libs/modules/ai-governance/src/services/pare/pare.service.ts` (targeted sections — `DataSanitizerService` instantiation, `zdrConfirmed` assignment), `apps/api/prisma/schema.prisma`'s `OpsAiAuditLog` model, a repo-wide search for `ZDR` (code) and `.env.example`/`.env.docker` for any documented status.

### The central question this story exists to answer

**Is DataSanitizer broken at its source, or is PARE simply consuming a correct implementation incorrectly? Confirmed: broken at its source, in both implementations found in the codebase.**

**Implementation 1 — `pare/data-sanitizer.service.ts`'s `DataSanitizerService`, the one `PAREService` actually instantiates and uses (`pare.service.ts:55`, `this.sanitizer = new DataSanitizerService()`).** Its `anonymizeName` method (`:67-73`) produces output like `"J. [SURNAME]"` or `"J. [NAME]"` — **not** the AC's confirmed `"Client A"` string at all. Its `isNameField` check (`:58-65`) only fires for the exact keys `clientname`, `contactname`, `clientcontact`, `guestname` — **there is no `supplierName` check anywhere in this class**, meaning supplier names pass through completely unredacted despite being a named AC requirement. There is also **no company-name handling** (no `"Corporate Client"` string appears anywhere in this file) and **no financial-amount handling** of any kind.

**Implementation 2 — `ai-governance.service.ts`'s separate, disconnected `sanitizePayload`/`runDryRun` flow**, which `PAREService` does **not** use (confirmed — PARE imports `DataSanitizerService` from the `pare/` subdirectory, not `AiGovernanceService`). This one does produce `"Client A"` for a `clientName`/`client_name` key match, and `"Supplier X"` for `supplierName`/`supplier_name` — closer to the AC in output shape, but its **string-level fallback redaction is hardcoded to two literal sample values**: `.replace(/\bAna Reyes\b/g, 'Client A')` and `.replace(/\bSonicLine Technicals\b/g, 'Supplier X')` (`:45-46`). Any real client or supplier name other than these two exact strings, appearing anywhere other than under an exactly-named key, passes through completely unredacted. Neither implementation handles company names or formats financial amounts as the AC specifies, and neither produces `"Supplier X - [category]"` (the category suffix is absent from both).

**Both implementations fail the AC's exact confirmed replacement rules, in different ways, and neither is the "PARE just wired it wrong" case the story's brief asked me to rule out.** PA-205/206 are **not** narrow, PARE-specific wiring bugs — they are foundational EP-15-S05-level defects. Any future AI feature that reuses either sanitizer inherits the same broken redaction regardless of how carefully it's wired into its own pipeline stage.

**New finding: PA-238 (CRITICAL).** DataSanitizer's redaction logic is broken at its source in both implementations found in the codebase — see above. This definitively resolves the story's central open question: the defect is foundational, not PARE-specific.
