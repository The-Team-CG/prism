# Changelog

Notable project changes are recorded here for people upgrading or contributing.
The format follows [Keep a Changelog 1.1.0](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/),
and the project follows [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/spec/v2.0.0.html).

## [Unreleased]

### Added

- The client's home, the supplier's Today page and both event overviews open with the same dark card over the event's cover: the step or event, its event and date, one large countdown (days overdue, left or to go), and a footer with what to do beside its button. An event's overview no longer repeats its title above that card, and its sections share one card style laid out so cards side by side end on the same line. For PMCM, the card shows the stage, when the client last opened the portal with their unread notices ("Ana Reyes last opened the portal 5 days ago · 2 notices unread"), the portal status and guest code. "Needs you" lists everything the Inbox has waiting on the event plus a venue visit the client asked for, under a bar of how many are urgent, soon, to check and to plan; each line says how long it has waited or when it is due ("waiting 6 days", "overdue 2 days"), in red once overdue or three days old, as it also does on the event cards. Readiness is a ring with what holds the score back, "Coming up" shows the event's dated work for the next 30 days as calendar leaves, and nine area tiles (including Billing, Files and Messages with their counts) are coloured by how each area stands. For the client, a "Get started" row covers a new event's first five steps, what is new since the last visit sits on the lead card, the floor plan card walks from their ideas to the approved layout, and the payments card shows the next amount due and how much is paid. The first visit after the event moves to a new stage says so once, and PMCM's internal "Latest update / Updated by" rows are gone. `GET /pmcm/event-attention?eventId=` returns one event's full list, and `GET /pmcm/events/:eventId/client-activity` returns the client's last sign-in and their unread notices about the event.
- Every event card has a picture. Events get a cover drawn from their type (corporate, conference, gala, product launch, celebration, workshop), and PMCM can make any image in an event's files its cover with "Use as event cover" (and take it off again with "Remove as event cover"); the cover then shows to the client, the booked suppliers and coordinators. PMCM's event tabs show how many events each holds, and each card has Billing, Files and Messages shortcuts with what is waiting there. A supplier's booking card shows when and where to arrive and the next payout, confirms a booking or acknowledges a schedule in one tap, and finished bookings move to Done. The client's next event lists the next dated things coming up. Events now carry their inquiry's type (`eventType`) and an optional `coverObjectId`, with `PATCH /events/:id/cover` for PMCM and `GET /events/:id/cover` for anyone who can read the event. The `portal-uat` seed gives each event a different type and adds an on-track event with a second XPERTO booking.
- Events and bookings are cards instead of tables. Each card shows the event day as a date tile, how far away it is, a six-step stage track from inquiry to closure, what is waiting and the money. PMCM Events opens on "Needs you": each card lists the Inbox's own items for that event (so the list and the Inbox always agree), each opening its work. Then come Coming up and Done, with Archive as one more tab in the same row. A supplier's bookings lead with the next step and a button that does it, progress through the steps and the money PMCM has released; the full checklist opens in a side panel, full screen on a phone. A client sees their next event large with the days to go and their next step, and their other events as cards. A new `GET /pmcm/event-attention` returns the Inbox's items grouped by event.
- Notifications are one screen on every portal. It opens on what asks the reader to confirm receipt, with its deadline and an Acknowledge button, then lists every notice by day (Today, Yesterday, then dates) with its event, the time, an unread dot and a link to the exact item. Clicking a notice opens it and marks it read; Mark all read, an Unread filter and "Show older notifications" sit above and below the list. Channel settings (email and Viber) moved into a side panel. PMCM gets a Notifications page and navigation item instead of a Settings tab, and clients, suppliers, coordinators and PMCM see an unread count on the Notifications navigation item. A coordinator's notice opens their shift or run sheet, not a PMCM event page. The inbox API now returns the event name, the unread total and a page size the reader can grow, and the portal-uat seed gives Ana, XPERTO, Ms. Mys and Marco notices to try it with.
- The notices sent with a billing statement, one per payment milestone, now open the event's Billing page.
- A client's How to pay is a dialog (a bottom sheet on a phone) with the amount to send, the reference to write, and a tab for each account PMCM has turned on. Each account has its own copy buttons for the name and number, its instructions, and its QR with a Save QR button (a phone cannot scan its own screen, so the QR is saved and opened from the banking or wallet app). It says when PMCM changed its details after the statement was issued, and when nothing has been set up it sends the client to PMCM instead of showing an account.
- Billing now uses PMCM's payment details. A client's statement of account shows the accounts PMCM has turned on now, never the copy printed on an older statement, and says when PMCM changed them after the statement was issued. A statement is issued with a copy of them and cannot be issued while none is turned on: the Billing sources tab warns and links to Payment details, and the placeholder accounts ("PMCM corporate bank account" and the like) are gone, as is the per-source list of payment methods. The statement of account PDF prints the live accounts and, for an account with a QR, where to scan it. A client can load a QR only for their own event once the statement is sent, and only while an account that is turned on still shows it. The migration `20261118090000_commercial_statement_payment_details_revision` adds a nullable column to billing statements, and the UAT scenario seeds payment details with a stand-in QR.
- Finance has a Payment details page where PMCM enters the accounts clients are told to pay to: bank accounts, GCash or Maya and a cheque payee, each with instructions, an on/off switch, an order, and a QR image for accounts. Edits are explicit: a bar shows unsaved changes, changing an existing account number asks for confirmation first, and if someone else saved in the meantime the save is refused with who did it. A preview shows what the client will see. Nothing a client sees changes yet; that follows in the next changes.
- PMCM can store one set of payment details for the company: bank accounts, e-wallets (GCash, Maya) and a cheque payee, each with optional instructions, and a QR image for accounts. The API is in place (`/api/payment-details`, PMCM finance only): saves check the revision the editor loaded, an unchanged save is not a new version, and QR images are PNG or JPEG up to 1 MB, stored once per image. The Finance screen to edit them, and clients seeing them, follow in the next changes. The migration `20261117090000_commercial_pmcm_payment_details` adds two commercial tables.
- Suppliers can confirm a payout reached them ("I received this") on Payments and attach their official receipt then or later. PMCM gets a notice. Each release on PMCM's supplier bookings shows whether the supplier confirmed it, with a download for their official receipt. Payouts that PMCM reversed cannot be confirmed. The migration `20261116090000_commercial_supplier_payout_receipts` adds four nullable columns to `commercial.supplier_payout_records`.
- Each client payment on PMCM's event billing page has a History button. It opens one panel (full screen on a phone) with everything that happened to the payment, oldest first: when it was scheduled, reminders, payments the client reported, money PMCM recorded, verified, did not accept, applied or took off, questions the client raised and how they were answered, and when it was paid in full. Each entry says who did it and when, and links to its receipt.
- Calendars colour payments by state on every portal: green when paid, red when late, orange when still due, with the state named on the entry. A client's "How to pay" shows the amount to send now, with a copy button. That amount is everything overdue, or else the next payment, less amounts under review, transfers still being checked and credit PMCM holds. Once an event is paid in full, its billing page lists every receipt with a download beside each, plus the statement of account.
- The PMCM Finance desk does the quick jobs in place. Its queue has search and filters by kind of work (client money, disputes, suppliers, setup) with counts. It marks work that arrived since the person's last visit. Payments can be verified straight from the queue, one at a time or several at once with one note (each client is told). Late clients can be reminded from their row. A "What clients owe" chart shows the outstanding balance by how late it is, and a cash outlook shows money due in from clients and out to suppliers each week for eight weeks, with late money in its own column. Both charts have a table view. "Download spreadsheet" exports every client payment with its amount, what is paid, being checked or disputed, and days late.
- Billing documents look like P.R.I.S.M documents: the billing statement, the statement of account and each payment receipt are branded PDFs the API renders (line items, payment schedule with status chips, payment history, how to pay with the reference to use, and a "Paid in full" or "Received" stamp). PMCM sees a payment's proof inline on the receipt, with Verify beside it, and a client sees a thumbnail of the proof before sending it.
- Billing tells the other side when something changes. The client gets a notice when PMCM accepts a payment they reported or does not accept a payment (with the reason); PMCM gets a notice that opens a payment the client reported on the receipts desk; a supplier gets a notice when PMCM sends money. PMCM can remind the client about a late or soon-due payment (once a day, logged on the payment). Billing shows overdue payments in the client sidebar, Finance shows the desk's open work in the PMCM sidebar, and Payments marks money a supplier has not seen yet.
- The `portal-uat` seed includes every billing state (paid in full, overdue and disputed, part paid with a payment to verify and a rejected transfer, unpaid with unapplied money, a draft source) and a late supplier payout, and locks the selected supplier quotes so payouts can be scheduled against them.
- Supplier Payments leads with what PMCM has paid, the next payment and what is still to come (and whether any is late), then shows each booking by event with the agreed amount and its payouts on a bar and a timeline, each with the transfers sent. A booking whose schedule PMCM has not set yet is still listed, and a supplier missing a payment can message PMCM from the page.
- PMCM Finance opens on one desk across every event instead of an event picker: totals for clients overdue, money to verify or apply, what PMCM owes suppliers, and open disputes and corrections; a "Needs you" queue in order of urgency (each item links to where it is handled); each event's client payments as a bar; and every supplier payout with its progress. The command inbox now also lists payments to check (marking ones the client reported), disputes, corrections and late supplier payouts, and shows client payments only once they are overdue or due within two weeks. An event's billing page leads with what is overdue or due next, shows whether each payment's money has been verified, folds the statement, reconciliation and delivery log away, and downloads the stored statement PDF (it previously always said the PDF was not ready). Receipts open straight from a link with totals that no longer change with the list filter. Billing sources take several line items and list drafts awaiting approval first.
- Client billing is one page across all events: what is overdue and due in the next two weeks first (net of amounts under review), each event's payments as a progress bar, and every payment on a timeline with its state. An event's billing page leads with how much is paid and the one thing to know now, shows how to pay from the statement, and lets the client report a payment with proof ("I've paid"). PMCM checks reported payments before they count; until then the client sees them as being checked, and sees a payment PMCM did not accept with PMCM's reason. The old Statements, Payment schedule, Reminders, Receipts and Balance tabs and their pages are gone, and the client home shows one payment row per event.
- Conversation messages can carry up to 10 files, uploaded from a device or copied from the event's Files library; each attached file is a copy that only the conversation's active members can open.
- Assigned coordinators can read and reply in conversations they belong to without starting new ones.
- PMCM can add and remove conversation members after a conversation starts (added people get a notice and see earlier messages), and close or reopen a conversation. Closed conversations take no new messages and move to a Closed list.
- Messages refresh every 15 seconds while the tab is visible without losing the open conversation, and the Messages navigation item shows an unread count (inside an event, that event's count). PMCM can see which members have read its messages; other roles' responses do not expose those read times.
- Assigned coordinators can read and reply in conversations they belong to without starting new ones. PMCM can see which members have read its messages; other roles' responses do not expose those read times.

- Chat notices include a link back to the recipient's conversation that survives sign-in. Messages offers Viber linking when configured, and the notification inbox combines channel copies while retaining each delivery's receipt checks.

- Signed-in client, supplier, PMCM/coordinator and guest-pass pages share a soft pastel mascot-and-doodle background, with opaque cards and headers keeping event work readable.

- Clients can follow event milestones, submit briefing and layout preferences, review official floorplans, sign conformes and addenda, track billing, and request an ocular visit. PMCM retains ownership of official plans and event decisions.
- PMCM can approve or reject the files clients send for a file requirement, with a reason, from the requirements panel, the same way as supplier files. The client or supplier who sent the file gets a notice with the outcome and, on a rejection, the reason; the reminder sweep no longer repeats that rejection notice.
- PMCM is told when a client or supplier sends a file for a requirement, and each file waiting for review appears in the command inbox until PMCM decides. A newer upload replaces the sender's earlier unreviewed file, so only the newest one waits. Notices show their whole message instead of a cut-off preview.
- PMCM's event Files tab opens on a library: files waiting for review come first (preview, approve, reject with a reason, or approve several at once), then one searchable table of every file from the client, suppliers and PMCM with who sent it, what it was for and where its review stands. Uploads that never finished are listed separately instead of among the files.
- Supplier Files shows what PMCM still needs first (drop a file on an item to send it; contracts link to their booking), then one searchable list of every booking document, contract version and certification, with PMCM's review state, expiry warnings and an inline preview.
- Stored files can carry a "valid until" date, set by whoever sent the file or by PMCM. Client and PMCM libraries flag files expiring within 30 days or already expired (judged by Philippine calendar day, so the valid-until day itself is still valid), "Use existing" won't resend an expired document, a copy keeps its original's date, and the requirement reminder sweep tells the sender (and PMCM) once before a required file expires and once after.
- Uploads that never finished (requested or failed and untouched for three days) are removed every six hours, with their partial provider bytes, so they stop filling file lists; a later retry of the same upload still resumes. `PRISM_DISABLE_ABANDONED_UPLOAD_CLEANUP_JOB=1` turns the sweep off.
- A requirement's upload history reads an earlier upload still waiting for review as replaced once the same sender sent a newer one, including uploads made before replacements were recorded, and the command inbox lists only each supplier's newest waiting file. Supplier booking documents show their valid-until date, which the supplier can set on files they sent.
- PMCM can plan events with versioned task templates, previewed date changes, supplier readiness and file requirements, event-day schedules, coordinator handoffs, and archive closeout records.
- PMCM finance can reconcile external receipts, apply and reverse payments, record refunds, review billing disputes, and attach private payment evidence. Clients can view published statements with payment and refund history.
- Guests can check in with consent, submit feedback within the event’s seven-day feedback window, and play event games. PMCM controls game sessions, moderation, retention, and event-scoped access.
- Signed-in users can view and acknowledge their own notices. PMCM can inspect provider outcomes, retry failed delivery, and escalate notices that miss acknowledgment deadlines. See the [operational communications runbook](docs/runbooks/operational-communications.md).

### Fixed

- PMCM's "Share a file" on an event's Files page works. Storage refused every file PMCM shared ("Storage owner reference is not supported for this usage"); a file PMCM shares is now accepted from PMCM only and visible to the client, not to suppliers or coordinators.
- "Show older notifications" stops at the 200 newest notices instead of failing with an error after six clicks, and a notification list that cannot load now says so and offers Try again instead of saying there are no notifications.
- Billing balances reflect recorded payments, and signing a conforme no longer creates fabricated billing. Idempotent command replays do not duplicate event decisions, audit records, notifications, or event-day logs.
- Audit hash verification matches PostgreSQL JSONB ordering and accepts valid legacy chains. A mismatched historical chain still blocks writes and requires integrity review; see the [audit-chain repair runbook](docs/runbooks/audit-chain-repair.md) before repair.
- Public and role-scoped responses enforce event access and omit private PMCM details. Sensitive mutations require a verified actor and event scope; authentication and public entry points have rate limits and session protections.

### Security

- Privacy exports, corrections, and account erasure require approved, scoped requests. Legal holds block matching erasure, staff account erasure is refused, and audit records retain metadata rather than exported record contents.
- File access checks event scope, visibility, content, and object identity. Payment evidence is PMCM-only; signed storage URLs must match the configured project and exact object path.

### Removed

- Broad sample API routes and browser-local demo fallbacks are retired. Guarded local fixture seeding remains available.
- Unused API ports, DTOs, portal primitives, package dependencies, and UI exports were removed without changing supported user workflows.
